About the Studio

The Nance Notary Services Executive Office & Learning Studio is a private educational resource for learning Florida notary procedures — clear lessons, a searchable document library, and practical checklists, all verified against primary Florida sources.

What this studio is

A welcoming place to build confidence in Florida notary procedures: acknowledgments, jurats, oaths, identification rules, fees, recordkeeping, and more — one clear lesson at a time.

What this studio is not

It is not a Florida government website, not a state-approved course, and not a source of legal advice. For advice about your particular circumstances, consult a licensed attorney. Read the full disclaimers.
