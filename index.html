Start Here: Your Learning Path

New to Florida notary work? Follow these steps in order. Each one points to the right folder in the Document Library and the matching video lesson.

  1. Florida Notary Foundations. Begin in Folder 01 — Start Here: Florida Notary Foundations to learn what a Florida notary public is, what a notarial act is, and the limits of the role.
  2. Signer Identification & Personal Appearance. Continue to Folder 05 — Signer Identification and Personal Appearance. Correct identification is the foundation of every act you perform.
  3. Acknowledgment vs. Jurat. Watch the pilot lesson “Acknowledgment vs. Jurat”, then open Folder 02 and Folder 03 for step-by-step guides to each act.
  4. Completing the Certificate. Learn to complete a notarial certificate correctly, every time — the wording, the seal, and the journal entry.
  5. Fees, Records & Refusals. Study Folder 11 — Fees, Receipts, and Business Records and Folder 12 — Refusals, Errors, and Difficult Situations so you know when to say no.
  6. Keep Current. Bookmark Folder 14 — Official Florida Sources and Legal Updates and check it whenever requirements may have changed.

Meet Your AI Presenter

AI-generated presenter · Educational use only

Your guide through the studio is an AI educational presenter for Nance Notary Services. She speaks directly to you, walks through each procedure step by step, and keeps every lesson friendly, confident, and welcoming.

Important: Notarial acts are performed by an appropriately authorized human notary — not by this AI presenter. She teaches; she does not notarize.

Every lesson opens or closes with this reminder: “For educational purposes only. I am a Florida notary public, not an attorney. This video does not provide legal advice.”
